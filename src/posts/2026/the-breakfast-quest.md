I launched a new site recently and added a reference to it on the [sites](/about/sites) page. Its called [The Breakfast Quest](https://thebreakfastquest.com/){target="_blank"} and my wife and I started it to write about the diners and greasy spoon restaurants we visit when we're traveling. 

![The Breakfast Quest home page](/images/sites/breakfastquest-800.png)

We basically write reviews of restaurants we visit and include some pictures and background on the restaurant. There's a [Locations](https://thebreakfastquest.com/locations/){target="_blank"} page that maps out all the locations we've visited. I generated a JSON data file automatically from posts and use it to generate the map, I'll write about that here soon.
